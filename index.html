Jatas AI (GuniVox)

Voice AI for university admissions. Co-developer, Dec 2025 – Jul 2026. TomatoVation’s flagship platform.

PythonFastAPISpeech-to-textText-to-speechLLMs

The problem

A telecaller makes thousands of calls. At that volume people make mistakes, forget follow-ups, and enter details incorrectly or not at all. Those gaps cost admissions.

What I did

  • Restarted a stalled project and, after researching the use case, redirected it to the harder outbound-calling problem. I rebuilt the prototype overnight.
  • Built backend and workflow automation (FastAPI) connecting speech-to-text, text-to-speech and an LLM, so follow-ups and data entry no longer depend on memory.
  • Ran pre-launch call tests for reliability, tone and response quality, and shaped the telecaller interface and training.
  • Was involved in the signing of the Ganpat University contract.

The result

Launched on schedule as the first live deployment, for Ganpat University’s admissions. I personally operated it through 1,000+ calls over 3 days. GuniVox received a ₹2,45,000 SSIP 2.0 product-development grant, approved in April 2026 for a three-member team.

ERP management system

Internal operations platform. Solo designer and developer, 2026. Built for TomatoVation.

The problem

A growing team needed one place for attendance, work logs, quotations, invoices, leave, payroll and task approvals.

What I did

  • Designed, built and deployed the system on Hostinger, using AI-assisted development (Google Antigravity, Claude).

Mental health sentiment analyzer

NLP research project. Developer, 2025. Presented at Research Conclave 2025.

PythonNLPXGBoostscikit-learn

The problem

Spotting emotional patterns in written text across seven categories: anxiety, depression, stress, suicidal ideation, bipolar, personality disorder and normal.

What I did

Built the preprocessing pipeline, then expanded about 50–60K public text records to 112,091 using oversampling and sentence augmentation. Generated sentence embeddings with all-MiniLM-L6-v2 and compared an XGBoost classifier against a Logistic Regression baseline.

What I’m improving

I am re-running the evaluation with the train and test split made before augmentation, so the reported accuracy can be trusted.
